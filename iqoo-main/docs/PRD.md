# 📄 Product Requirements Document (PRD) — BharatPay SuperApp

**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Prepared For:** FinTech Hackathon & Startup Pitch  
**Tagline:** *"Apka Paisa, Apka Bhasha, Apka Bharat"* (Your Money, Your Language, Your India)  

## 1. Executive Summary
Build India's most inclusive, AI-powered FinTech and Commerce SuperApp that seamlessly integrates money, payments, commerce, lending, investing, and financial inclusion — designed specifically for how India actually transacts across Tier 1, 2, 3 cities and rural Bharat.

## 2. 10 Revolutionary Features
1. **Voice-First Vernacular Commerce** (Bhashini ASR/NLU, code-switching).
2. **AI Financial Saathi** (Personalized advisor, expense tracking, predictive EMI advice).
3. **Gamified Financial Literacy** (50+ modules, quizzes, coin rewards, certificates).
4. **ONDC Local Commerce Network** (2km radius, 0% commission, live GPS delivery).
5. **Community Chit Funds 2.0** (Blockchain trust ledger, live reverse auction bidding).
6. **Unified Multi-Asset Investment** (Direct MFs, 24K Gold, ETFs, NPS, Goal Baskets).
7. **Instant Micro-Credit & Bharat Score** (Alternative data XGBoost ML, 30s disbursal).
8. **AI Fraud Shield** (Deepfake voice detection, phishing URL scanner, Emergency freeze).
9. **Phygital Merchant Network** (3,750+ Saathi touchpoints, IoT Soundbox announcer).
10. **Feature Phone Parity** (USSD *99#, IVR 1800 voice banking, SMS, WhatsApp).
