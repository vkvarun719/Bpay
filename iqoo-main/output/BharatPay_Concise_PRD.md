# 📄 Product Requirements Document (Concise Version) — BharatPay SuperApp

**Tagline:** *"Apka Paisa, Apka Bhasha, Apka Bharat"* (Your Money, Your Language, Your India)

## Executive Summary
BharatPay SuperApp is an AI-powered, vernacular-native FinTech & Commerce platform specifically architected for how 600M+ Indians in Tier 2, 3, 4 cities and rural Bharat transact daily.

## 10 Core Features Built:
1. **Voice-First Vernacular Commerce:** "Bolo aur kharido" in 12+ Indian languages with Bhashini NLU.
2. **AI Financial Saathi:** Conversational personal finance assistant with proactive budget and loan guidance.
3. **Gamified Literacy (Shiksha):** 50+ bite-sized learning lessons, quiz engine, and shareable certificates.
4. **ONDC Local Commerce:** 2km hyperlocal Kirana store directory with 0% platform commission and live delivery tracking.
5. **Community Chit Funds 2.0:** Digitized chit funds on transparent blockchain ledger with live reverse auction bidding.
6. **Unified Multi-Asset Wealth:** Direct Mutual Funds (0% commission), 24K MMTC-PAMP Gold, ETFs, NPS, and Goal Baskets.
7. **Instant Micro-Credit:** Alternative XGBoost credit scoring engine with 30-sec instant loan disbursal.
8. **AI Fraud Shield:** SEBI Problem Statement #1 aligned deepfake voice analyzer, phishing URL scanner, and 1-click freeze.
9. **Phygital Touchpoints:** 3,750+ Saathi touchpoints directory + IoT Soundbox voice announcer.
10. **Feature Phone Parity:** Interactive USSD `*99#`, IVR 1800, SMS Banking, and WhatsApp Bot.
